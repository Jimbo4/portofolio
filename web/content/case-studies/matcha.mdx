## Real dates, not endless chats

Matcha starts from a simple observation: the good stuff doesn't happen in chat. Swipe apps keep people matching and messaging for weeks, and very few of those conversations ever turn into a meeting.

On Matcha, a match goes straight to a table. Both people say when they're free, pay for their drink in the app, and Matcha books the date at one of its partner venues in Milan. Each date costs €10 per person, drink included.

## A brand, a product and a market to build from zero

I co-founded Matcha with the CEO and lead product, design and marketing. We started from a blank page and needed:

- **A name and an identity** that feel warm and direct, and look nothing like gamified swipe apps.
- **A core flow** where every match ends at a real table, with an in-app payment as the moment people commit.
- **A two-sided marketplace**, with singles on one side and a network of partner venues on the other.
- **A B2C launch** for people aged 25–40, with a focus on women and the LGBT+ community.

## From naming to launch: how Matcha came together

I cover the full product cycle, from naming and branding to the core flow, the store assets and the launch content.

<Disciplines>
  <Discipline label="Brand identity">
    Naming, wordmark and visual identity. The palette uses solid colors (mint, aqua, pink and yellow on charcoal), flower shapes and halftone photography to set Matcha apart from neon swipe-app aesthetics. The same system runs from the app to the store pages and social media.
  </Discipline>
  <Discipline label="Core flow">
    The path from match to a first date paid in-app. People pick time slots on at least five different days, and Matcha finds a venue at a time that works for both. The chat stays locked until two hours before the date and is meant for last-minute logistics only, so the conversation happens at the table.
  </Discipline>
  <Discipline label="Profile setup">
    A short, five-step setup where the profile is the product. At least two photos, interests and personal prompts, and a preview of how others will see you before you publish. A small, deliberate amount of friction that raises the quality of every match.
  </Discipline>
  <Discipline label="Trust and compliance">
    I turned GDPR and consumer law constraints into product requirements. Dates happen in verified public venues, reporting someone also blocks them, and a no-show gives the other person a credit for their next date.
  </Discipline>
  <Discipline label="Partner venues">
    With the CEO, I built the partner venue network through field sales and an AI-assisted CRM: ten venues so far. For launch, bookings run on a lean, manual-first ops process instead of custom tooling, so we could start small and learn quickly.
  </Discipline>
  <Discipline label="Metrics and experiments">
    I defined the north star metrics and the flow variants to test, so every iteration of the funnel has a clear goal and a way to measure it.
  </Discipline>
</Disciplines>

## A content engine built with AI

I lead B2C marketing. To keep up with a content calendar without a studio, I built the repositories for an AI-assisted content pipeline. Every Instagram post is written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript on top of the brand tokens, then rendered into ready-to-publish images, so copy and visuals can change as quickly as the ideas.

## Getting people off their phones

Matcha puts product, brand and go-to-market in one system. The name and identity make it feel different at first glance. The core flow makes the meeting the point of the app, the venue network supplies the tables, and the content pipeline reaches the audience in the brand's voice.
